About

Halewood Systems is an independent software studio based in the European Union.

We make tools we want to use ourselves: fast, simple and honest. No bundled extras, no dark patterns, no selling your data.

Our first product, Windrova, brings professional weather models to everyone, free. Next comes Kyrofix, PC care software that explains what it does. We also make games, like Slipgate.
