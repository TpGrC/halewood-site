How to play

1

Tap anywhere to drop the sliding block.

2

Anything hanging over the edge falls off.

3

Six perfect drops in a row make the tower bigger again.

On iPhone: open the game in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". On Android: Chrome menu, then "Install app".
