Activate Kyrofix Pro

Turn on Pro with your key after you buy.

  1. After you buy, your receipt email has a licence key.
  2. Go to halewoodsystems.com/activate, paste that key and click the button. You get your Kyrofix key (it starts with KYRO).
  3. Open the Kyrofix app, click Pro, paste the KYRO key and click Activate.

New PC?

One key works on up to 3 PCs. If you need more, email us.
