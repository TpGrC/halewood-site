Back up your files

Keep a copy of your photos and documents on a USB drive.

  1. Plug in a USB drive or external disk.
  2. Open Kyrofix Backup and pick the folders to save. Documents, Desktop and Pictures are chosen for you.
  3. Pick the USB drive and click Back up now.

Get files back

Open Kyrofix Backup, click Restore, pick the backup and choose where to put the files.
