Free up disk space

A quick, safe way to make room on a full drive.

  1. Run Kyrofix Cleaner and clean junk files. This is always safe.
  2. Open Kyrofix Space and scan your drive. The biggest folders are at the top.
  3. Look in Downloads and Videos first. Old installers and videos are often the biggest.
  4. Use Photo Cleaner to remove duplicate photos.

Everything you delete goes to the Recycle Bin first, so you can get it back.
