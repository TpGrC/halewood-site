Play games offline on your phone

Add a Halewood game to your home screen so it opens like an app.

iPhone

  1. Open the game in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

Android

  1. Open the game in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots), then Install app or Add to Home screen.

Open it once with internet. After that it works offline. Your coins and progress are saved on your phone.
