Domande

Is it safe to install?

Yes. Kyrofix installs only itself, with no toolbars or extra programs, and you can remove it any time in Windows Settings, Apps.

Why does Windows show a SmartScreen warning?

New programs from small studios are not yet known to SmartScreen. Click "More info" and then "Run anyway". The warning goes away as more people use the app.

Is the free version really free?

Yes, for as long as you want. Pro adds extra features, it does not unlock basic ones.

How do I activate Pro?

After buying, open halewoodsystems.com/activate, paste the licence key from your receipt and copy your Kyrofix key into the app, under Pro. One key works on up to 3 of your PCs.

Yes, within 14 days of buying, even if you already activated the key. Email support@halewoodsystems.com.
