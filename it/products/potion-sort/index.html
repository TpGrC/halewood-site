How to play

1

Tap a bottle to pick it up, tap another to pour.

2

You can only pour onto the same colour or into an empty bottle.

3

Finish when every bottle holds one colour.

On iPhone: open the game in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". On Android: Chrome menu, then "Install app".
