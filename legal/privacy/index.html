Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

We collect as little personal data as possible. This policy explains what we process, why, and your rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Who is responsible

Halewood Systems, Greece, EU. Contact: support@halewoodsystems.com.

What we process

Visiting our websites

Our sites are hosted on GitHub Pages and use Cloudflare for some services. These providers process technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the pages and keep them secure. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not identify you.

Windrova

When you look up a forecast, the map position is sent to our weather relay and to public weather services (such as MET Norway) to get the data. Your settings (language, units, favourite places) are stored only in your own browser. Place search uses the Photon service.

Emails

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to answer you, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Payments and donations

Payments and donations are handled by Stripe. Stripe processes your payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive only what we need for our records, such as the amount and date.

Legal bases

  • Providing a service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
  • Our legitimate interest in running secure, working websites (Art. 6(1)(f))
  • Legal obligations, such as keeping invoices (Art. 6(1)(c))

Transfers outside the EU

Some providers (for example GitHub, Cloudflare and Stripe) may process data outside the EU. They use safeguards approved by the European Commission, such as Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep data

Only as long as needed for the purpose, or as long as the law requires (for example, tax records).

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to receive your data in a portable format. Email support@halewoodsystems.com. You can also complain to a data protection authority; in Greece this is the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr).

Children

Our services are not aimed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
