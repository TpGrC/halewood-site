Refunds & withdrawal

Last updated: 19 November 2026

All our products currently have free versions. This page explains your rights when paid plans become available.

14-day right of withdrawal

If you are a consumer in the EU, you can withdraw from a purchase within 14 days without giving a reason (Directive 2011/83/EU). The 14 days start on the day you buy.

Digital content you start using right away

For software and digital content delivered immediately, the law allows the right of withdrawal to end once delivery starts, but only if you agreed to this at checkout and confirmed you understand you lose the right. We will always ask you clearly before that happens.

Subscriptions

You can cancel a subscription at any time. It stops at the end of the current paid period. If you withdraw within 14 days of your first payment, before you start using paid features, we refund you in full.

If something doesn't work

If a product does not work as described, you are entitled to have it fixed, or to a price reduction or refund, under EU law on digital content (Directive (EU) 2019/770). This applies regardless of the 14-day period.

How to ask

Email support@halewoodsystems.com with the email address you used and the date of purchase. You can also use this sentence: "I hereby give notice that I withdraw from my contract for the following product: …". We refund within 14 days, to the same payment method, at no cost to you.

Donations

Donations are voluntary gifts and are not purchases. If you donated by mistake, contact us within 14 days and we will refund it.
