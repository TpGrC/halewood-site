Five free games you can play right now

15 November 2026

From merging planets to sorting potions: five quick games that run on any phone or computer.

No download, no account. Open a game and play. On your phone, add it to your home screen and it works offline.

  • Starfuse: drop and merge planets until you make a black hole.
  • Potion Sort: pour until every bottle holds one colour.
  • Triple Bloom: match three tiles to clear the garden.
  • Skystack: tap at the right moment to build a tower.
  • Slipgate: slide every block out through its own gate. 200 levels.

Every puzzle level is checked by a computer before it ships, so it can always be solved.
