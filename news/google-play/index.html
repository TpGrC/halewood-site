Our games are coming to Google Play

20 November 2026

Starfuse, Potion Sort, Triple Bloom, Skystack and Slipgate are getting real Android apps. Here is what changes and when.

All five Halewood games are getting their own Android apps on Google Play: Starfuse, Potion Sort, Triple Bloom, Skystack and Slipgate. The Windrova weather app comes along with them.

What is different in the app?

  • It opens straight from your home screen, full screen.
  • Everything is inside the app, so it works with no internet at all.
  • Your progress, coins and stars stay on your phone.

When?

New apps on Google Play first go through a short closed test. After that they open to everyone. Until then, every game is free to play right now in your browser, on phone or computer.

Want to test early?

We are looking for Android testers. Email us the Gmail address you use on your phone and we will add you to the test.
