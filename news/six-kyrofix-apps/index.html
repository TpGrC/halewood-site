Kyrofix is now six apps

18 November 2026

Space, Shredder and Photo Cleaner join Driver Updater, Cleaner and Backup. All free to download.

Kyrofix started as a driver updater. It is now a small family of six Windows tools, each one doing one job well.

  • Driver Updater keeps your drivers up to date.
  • Cleaner removes junk and speeds up startup.
  • Backup copies your files to a USB drive.
  • Space shows what is filling your disk.
  • Shredder deletes files for good.
  • Photo Cleaner finds duplicate and blurry photos.

Same rules for all of them

No admin prompt to install, no toolbars, no extra programs. Every app has a free version with no time limit, and you can remove it in Windows Settings any time.
