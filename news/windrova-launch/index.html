Windrova: the weather map for the whole world

10 November 2026

Wind, rain, waves and three forecast models in one free map, in 13 languages.

Windrova is a live weather map you can use anywhere in the world, for free and with no account.

  • Wind, rain, temperature, waves, clouds and satellite.
  • Three forecast models side by side: ECMWF, GFS and GEM.
  • An activity planner that tells you the best time to go out.

Open windrova.com on your phone and add it to your home screen. The Android app is on its way.
