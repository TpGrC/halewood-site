Triple Bloom

A tile-matching puzzle in a garden. Tap a free tile to put it in your tray; three of the same disappear. Clear the board before the tray fills up.

How to play

1

Tap any tile that is not covered.

2

Three of the same in the tray disappear.

3

Clear every tile before your 7-slot tray is full.

On iPhone: open the game in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". On Android: Chrome menu, then "Install app".

Pytania

Is it free?

Yes. You can play every level for free. Optional ads give you extra coins, power-ups or a second chance.

Do I need to download anything?

No. It plays in your browser on phone or computer. To keep it on your phone, choose "Add to Home Screen" in Safari or "Install app" in Chrome.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device, in your browser. Clearing your browser data resets it.

Does it work offline?

Yes, after you have opened it once.
