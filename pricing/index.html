Questions

What do I lose if I stay on Free?

Nothing you already use. Free never expires and has no ads. Pro only adds the extra tools marked in the tables above.

Yearly or forever?

Yearly is cheaper to start. Forever is a one-time payment and never runs out. Both work on up to 3 PCs.

Do prices include VAT?

Yes. For customers in the EU, prices shown include VAT where it applies.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, under EU consumer law. See Refunds & withdrawal.

Can I cancel a subscription?

Yes, at any time. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
