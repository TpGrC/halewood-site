Questions

Is it free?

Yes. You can play every level for free. Optional ads give you extra coins, power-ups or a second chance.

Do I need to download anything?

No. It plays in your browser on phone or computer. To keep it on your phone, choose "Add to Home Screen" in Safari or "Install app" in Chrome.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device, in your browser. Clearing your browser data resets it.

Does it work offline?

Yes, after you have opened it once.
