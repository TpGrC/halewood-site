Frequently asked

Is Windrova really free?

Yes. All weather maps, models and the 10-day forecast are free, with no account and no ads.

When is the Windrova mobile app coming?

Soon. Until then, open windrova.com on your phone and use "Add to home screen". It works like an app.

Is Kyrofix free?

Yes. The free version updates your drivers with no time limit. Pro, with automatic updates and extras, is coming soon.

Something isn't working. What do I send?

Tell us which product, what you did, and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

How do I delete my data?

We keep almost no data. If you emailed us, ask and we'll delete your messages. See our Privacy policy.
